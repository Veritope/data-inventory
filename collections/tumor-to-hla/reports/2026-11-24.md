### What the exclusions tell us

Review articles can lead to primary papers but provide no new assayed people. A selected MDS case demonstrates an RNA fusion without measuring its frequency in a cohort. Several multi-cancer data releases reanalyze TCGA or DepMap, measure gene dependencies or expression signatures, or cover only a selected gene panel. A spatial imaging dataset measures translation activity through pS6 staining. That marker cannot identify which altered protein was translated. For both “PTM Immunopeptidomics” DOI records, the Zenodo landing page resolves to the same HLA **class II** file. The article follows classical HLA class I. Individual reasons and metadata sources are preserved for all 29 exclusions. [Class II deposit file listing](https://zenodo.org/api/records/17985996)

Screening used article abstracts and deposit metadata. A later paper version, an appropriate independent patient table, or new assay-level evidence can reopen its record. All six deferred items need primary extraction or verification before inclusion. A complete weekly screen can leave estimates unchanged.
